Frequently Asked Questions

What does Friends Tax Point do?

Friends Tax Point is a tax and business consultancy in Kerala, India, serving clients since 2001. We handle Income Tax Return (ITR) filing, GST registration and returns, TDS returns, digital signatures, e-way bills, business registration, accounting, Rubber Board returns and agricultural subsidy paperwork for individuals, farmers and businesses.

Where are Friends Tax Point offices located?

Friends Tax Point has 8 branches in Kerala: Anchal (Kuruvikonam), Ayoor (Malayil Building), Thingalkarikkom (Junction), Kilimanoor (MR Complex, SH 1), Pathanapuram (Puthuval), Venchempu (Pinacle Point), Enath (K.J. Building) and Ranni (near KSRTC bus stand), across Kollam, Thiruvananthapuram and Pathanamthitta districts.

What documents do I need to file my income tax return?

You usually need your PAN, Aadhaar, Form 16 (if salaried), bank statements, interest certificates and proof of investments or deductions. We'll send you a simple checklist for your situation.

How long does it take to file an ITR with Friends Tax Point?

Most income tax returns are filed within 24 hours of receiving complete documents. Our average response time to new enquiries is under 24 hours.

Can I file my return without visiting the office?

Yes. You can share documents by phone or email and we handle the full filing. You're also welcome to visit any of our 8 branches in Kerala.

Do you handle GST registration for new businesses?

Yes. We handle new GST registration, monthly and quarterly returns (GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B), annual returns and reconciliation.

Is agricultural income taxable in India?

Agricultural income is generally exempt from income tax in India, but it must still be reported correctly in your return. We help rubber planters and farmers report it properly and claim eligible subsidies.

I received a tax notice. Can you help?

Yes. Share the Income Tax or GST notice with us and our consultants will review it and prepare the right response for you.
