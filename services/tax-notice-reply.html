Questions about tax notices & planning

I received an income tax notice. What should I do first?

Do not ignore it, and do not panic. Note the section quoted and the deadline — most replies are due within 15 to 30 days. Send us the notice and we'll tell you what it means and what it will take to answer.

What happens if I miss the deadline to reply?

The department can proceed on its own assessment, raise a demand, add interest and penalty, and in some cases adjust your future refunds. Replying late is far more expensive than replying on time.

Can you respond on my behalf?

Yes. Most proceedings are now faceless and handled through the income tax portal, so we prepare the submission and file it with your authorisation. You don't need to visit an office.

How do I avoid notices in the future?

Most notices come from simple mismatches — income in your AIS that wasn't reported, TDS claimed that doesn't appear in 26AS, or deductions without proof. Filing from reconciled records removes almost all of them.
