Questions about salaried professionals

Which ITR form applies to me?

Most salaried people file ITR-1 (Sahaj). If you have capital gains, more than one house property, or foreign income, it becomes ITR-2. We pick the correct form for you — filing the wrong one can make the return defective.

Should I choose the old or the new tax regime?

It depends on your deductions. The new regime has lower rates but removes most exemptions. We calculate your tax both ways and file under whichever is cheaper for you that year.

I changed jobs and have two Form 16s. What happens?

Both have to be combined, because each employer gave you the basic exemption and deductions separately. That often leaves tax payable. We merge them properly so you don't get a notice later.

I missed the filing deadline. Can I still file?

Yes. A belated return can be filed until the end of the assessment year, with a late fee under section 234F. Bring your documents and we'll file it right away.
