Questions about freelancers & professionals

What is section 44ADA and does it apply to me?

It is a presumptive scheme for specified professionals with gross receipts up to the prescribed limit. You declare 50% of receipts as income, pay tax on that, and keep no detailed books. It helps when your actual expenses are below half your receipts — we check which way is better for you.

My client deducted TDS. How do I get it back?

The TDS appears in your Form 26AS. When your actual tax works out lower than the amount deducted, filing a return gets the difference refunded to your bank account, usually within a few weeks.

Do I have to pay advance tax?

If your tax for the year after TDS exceeds Rs 10,000, yes — in quarterly instalments. Missing them means interest under sections 234B and 234C. We work out a schedule at the start of the year.

Do freelancers need GST registration?

Only once your receipts cross the threshold for services, or if you supply inter-state or through an online platform. Export of services has its own rules, and we set up an LUT so you can bill overseas clients without IGST.
