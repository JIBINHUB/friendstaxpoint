Questions about companies & startups

How long does company registration take?

With complete documents, a private limited company or LLP is usually incorporated within one to two weeks, depending on name approval and MCA processing. Digital signatures take a day or two at the start.

How many people do I need to start?

A private limited company needs at least two directors and two shareholders. An LLP needs two partners. If you are on your own, a One Person Company or a proprietorship may suit you better — we'll explain the trade-offs.

What compliance follows after registration?

Every company files annual returns with the ROC, keeps books, holds board meetings and files income tax returns — whether or not it trades. Missing these brings penalties that grow daily, so we keep a calendar for you.

Can you prepare a project report for a bank loan?

Yes. We prepare project reports with financial projections in the format banks and schemes expect, based on realistic figures for your business.
