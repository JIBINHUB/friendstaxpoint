Questions about farmers & rubber planters

Is agricultural income taxable in India?

Pure agricultural income is exempt from income tax under section 10(1). It still has to be reported in your return, because the department uses it to work out the rate on your other income. Leaving it out is a common reason for notices.

How is income from rubber taxed?

Rubber is a composite income. Under Rule 7A, 35% of the income from growing and manufacturing rubber is treated as business income and taxed, while 65% is treated as agricultural and exempt. We compute this split for you.

What do I need for a replanting subsidy?

Generally your Rubber Board registration, land records, a planting or replanting application and field inspection records. We prepare the file and follow it through so nothing stalls.

I only have farm income. Do I still need to file a return?

Often yes — to claim a refund of TDS, to support a loan application, or simply to keep a clean record. We'll tell you honestly whether filing helps you.
