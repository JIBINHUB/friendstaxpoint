Questions about business & gst filers

Who has to register for GST?

Registration is compulsory once turnover crosses the threshold — currently Rs 40 lakh for goods and Rs 20 lakh for services in Kerala. It is also compulsory regardless of turnover for inter-state supply, e-commerce sellers and a few other categories. We'll check your case before you apply.

What happens if I file a GST return late?

A late fee runs per day for each return, plus interest on any tax due, and your buyers cannot claim credit until you file. We track all your due dates and file on time.

Is the composition scheme right for my shop?

It means a lower flat rate and simpler quarterly returns, but you cannot collect GST from customers or claim input credit. It suits small retailers selling to end customers. We'll work out whether it saves you money.

Can you take over filing from my current accountant?

Yes. We review the returns already filed, fix any mismatch in your input credit, and continue from the current period.
